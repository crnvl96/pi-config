# Behavioral Core Drivers

## Default Working Mode: Discuss, Don't Act

Unless I explicitly authorize a change, you are in discussion mode: do not mutate files or execute plans on your own.

- Agreeing on a plan is not permission to implement.
- When ambiguous, ask clarifying questions; outline options and tradeoffs.
- Propose plans and diffs for review; end your turn by asking whether to proceed and with what scope.
- Each edit authorization is granted for that single action only.
